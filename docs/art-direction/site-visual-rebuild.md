# JasonStu Apps site visual rebuild

Status: Current user-directed visual brief; this is not an approval record

Scope: Collection home, LinkScope Lite, Trackpad Wizard, support, and privacy
documents

Date: 2026-09-28

## Direction

Give the whole site the feel of an independent software maker's clear, carefully
edited product shelf. Share native sans typography, warm-white and ink neutrals,
blue links, common content edges, consistent heading rhythm, and fine section
rules. Use actual app captures as the main visual evidence. Keep product pages
distinct through their own names, descriptions, and authentic imagery while the
publisher shell and document pages remain recognizably related.

## Composition and content

- Keep the collection home as a clear catalogue: state who makes the apps, then
  give each app a readable editorial entry with purpose, availability, platform
  requirement, and direct routes. Preserve discoverability without a generic
  colored rounded feature-card grid.
- Give each product page its own opening, true product imagery, useful section
  sequence, and practical facts. LinkScope Lite communicates read-only device
  details, explicit availability, on-demand diagnostics, and six dashboard
  widget types. Trackpad Wizard keeps its own touch, gesture, and haptics story.
- Let support and privacy documents use the same navigation, type, links, width,
  and separators. Their substantive support and data-handling facts stay
  unchanged by visual work.
- Keep a shared centered width and align text, images, section headings, facts,
  and actions to deliberate common edges. Long-form documents stay comfortably
  narrow; avoid broad blank columns, tiny monospaced eyebrows, ordinal labels,
  design-explanation captions, and copy added only to fill space.
- Let authentic screenshot framing and app marks remain as captured. Do not add
  purple gradient fields, marketing-card treatments, numbered bubble steps, or
  simulated app behavior.

## Type, color, and responsive behavior

Use the native system sans stack throughout. Establish hierarchy with size,
weight, line height, and spacing rather than multiple typefaces or tiny labels.
Keep body copy comfortably readable and links clearly blue with visible keyboard
focus. The surrounding site uses warm white, ink, muted text, and subtle rules;
app-specific color remains in actual product artwork or captured UI.

On narrow viewports, reflow sections to one column in source order. Keep text,
actions, and screenshots readable and avoid horizontal page scrolling. Documents
must support 200% text resizing and 320 CSS-pixel reflow. Preserve Light/Dark
contrast, keyboard access, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Evidence and references

The user supplied the visual constraints above. The direction also draws on
primary guidance and independent maker sites:

- [Apple Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography): system typography, legibility, and hierarchy through a restrained set of type treatments.
- [Apple UI Design Dos and Don’ts](https://developer.apple.com/design/tips/): readable sizing and contrast, alignment, and fitting content to the available screen.
- [WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/): contrast, resize, reflow, and text-spacing criteria.
- [Shiina Labs](https://shiinalabs.com/): one independent maker presents varied software projects under a shared identity with distinct product and support routes.
- [Maciej Bula](https://maciejbula.dev/): concise, low-pitch catalogue of personal software and experiments.
- [Crafter Apps](https://www.crafterapps.com/): shared publisher identity connecting separate products and practical information routes.
- [Linkscope.io](https://linkscope.io/): negative positioning reference. Its indexed home presents a commercial link-building marketplace with publisher-volume claims, agency proof points, guarantees, and sales language. A direct page fetch timed out during research, so no specific visual-style claim is made here.

The LinkScope Lite screenshot source and capture limits remain recorded in
`DESIGN_MANUAL.md` and `docs/audits/linkscope-2.0.1.md`. This brief does not
change product behavior, support instructions, privacy statements, release facts,
routes, server rendering, or Cloudflare deployment.
